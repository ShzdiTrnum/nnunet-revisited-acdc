# nnU-Net Revisited on ACDC: an independent reproduction

Independent reproduction of the ACDC results in **nnU-Net Revisited** (Isensee et al., MICCAI 2024)
for five of the paper's configurations: three CNNs (the original nnU-Net, nnU-Net ResEnc M and ResEnc L),
one Mamba-based network (U-Mamba Bot) and one Transformer-based network (SwinUNETR).
Training and evaluation ran on 2× NVIDIA RTX 6000 Ada (48 GB) and follow the paper's protocol:
5-fold cross-validation on the ACDC training set, using nnU-Net's own patient-level splits.

> - The three nnU-Net models reproduce the paper's ACDC Dice within **0.03 points** (at most 0.06 of a fold
>   standard deviation) and its NSD within **0.08 points**.
> - U-Mamba Bot is within **0.16 Dice points** (0.28 fold SD) and **0.34 NSD points** of the paper.
> - SwinUNETR is **not reproduced**: 2.88 Dice points below the paper (2.7 fold SD), with the setup
>   differences listed under *Deviations*.

## Paper

- F. Isensee, T. Wald, C. Ulrich, M. Baumgartner, S. Roy, K. Maier-Hein, P. F. Jäger.
  *nnU-Net Revisited: A Call for Rigorous Validation in 3D Medical Image Segmentation.* MICCAI 2024.
  [arXiv:2404.09556](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.09556)
- Official code (Apache-2.0): <https://github.com/MIC-DKFZ/nnUNet>
- U-Mamba and SwinUNETR were trained with the official U-Mamba code (Apache-2.0):
  <https://github.com/bowang-lab/U-Mamba>

## What is reproduced, and what is not

| Item | Paper | This repo |
|---|---|---|
| Dataset | ACDC, 100 training patients (200 ED/ES volumes) | downloaded from the official CREATIS server |
| Models | nnU-Net (org.), ResEnc M / L / XL, MedNeXt, STU-Net, SwinUNETR, U-Mamba, ... | **nnU-Net (org.), ResEnc M, ResEnc L, U-Mamba Bot, SwinUNETR** |
| Configuration | `3d_fullres` | `3d_fullres` |
| Split | 5-fold CV, splits generated by nnU-Net | nnU-Net's official ACDC split (`create_ACDC_split`, patient-level, seed 1234), checked for leakage; the identical split file is used for all five models |
| Training | nnU-Net defaults (1000 epochs) | nnU-Net defaults for the CNNs and U-Mamba; U-Mamba's own SwinUNETR trainer (see *Deviations*) |
| Metrics | DSC (and NSD @ 2 mm), averaged over classes and folds | DSC per class (RV, MYO, LV), mean ± SD over folds; NSD @ 2 mm with the same library as the paper |
| Framework | nnU-Net v2 (version used in the paper not pinned here) | nnU-Net v2.8.1 for the CNNs; U-Mamba's bundled nnU-Net v2.1.1 for U-Mamba and SwinUNETR (see *Deviations*) |

Not reproduced: ResEnc XL, U-Mamba Enc, MedNeXt, STU-Net, SwinUNETRV2, nnFormer, CoTr, and the other
datasets in the paper.

## Results

### Main table (5-fold cross-validation, ACDC, %)

| Model | Type | Dice RV | Dice MYO | Dice LV | **Mean Dice** (± SD over folds) | Paper Dice | Gap to paper | NSD @ 2 mm | Paper NSD |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| nnU-Net (org.) | CNN | 90.12 | 89.96 | 94.61 | **91.56 ± 0.63** | 91.54 | +0.02 (0.03 SD) | 94.94 ± 0.73 | 94.93 |
| nnU-Net ResEnc M | CNN | 90.77 | 90.27 | 94.90 | **91.98 ± 0.57** | 91.99 | −0.01 (0.02 SD) | 95.53 ± 0.69 | 95.50 |
| nnU-Net ResEnc L | CNN | 90.50 | 89.97 | 94.69 | **91.72 ± 0.47** | 91.69 | +0.03 (0.06 SD) | 95.19 ± 0.55 | 95.11 |
| U-Mamba Bot | Mamba | 90.31 | 89.95 | 94.62 | **91.63 ± 0.58** | 91.79 | −0.16 (0.28 SD) | 95.06 ± 0.66 | 95.40 |
| SwinUNETR | Transformer | 85.52 | 86.67 | 93.04 | **88.41 ± 1.05** | 91.29 | **−2.88 (2.74 SD)** | 90.96 ± 1.07 | 94.12 |

Paper values: Dice from Table 1, NSD from Table 4. The paper reports class-averaged values only; the
per-class Dice is an extra output of this reproduction. Aggregation follows the paper: per fold, the mean
over cases and over the three classes; then mean ± SD over the five folds. `evaluate_all.py` computes Dice
independently and matches each model's own nnU-Net `summary.json` for every fold (max difference 0.000 points).
All five models were evaluated on exactly the same validation cases per fold (checked by the script).

### Comparing the models (applying the rule set out before training)

Before training I committed to not claiming a ranking between models unless the difference exceeds the
fold-to-fold SD (about 0.5–0.6 Dice points for the nnU-Net baseline). Paired on the same folds, against the
original nnU-Net:

| Comparison | Mean difference | Folds in favour | Paper difference | Larger than fold SD? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ResEnc M − nnU-Net (org.) | +0.42 | 5 / 5 | +0.45 | no |
| ResEnc L − nnU-Net (org.) | +0.16 | 4 / 5 | +0.15 | no |
| U-Mamba Bot − nnU-Net (org.) | +0.07 | 3 / 5 | +0.25 | no |
| SwinUNETR − nnU-Net (org.) | −3.15 | 0 / 5 | −0.25 | **yes** |
| ResEnc M − ResEnc L | +0.26 | 5 / 5 | +0.30 | no |

What this supports:

- **The three CNNs and U-Mamba Bot are within one fold SD of each other.** In particular, U-Mamba Bot is
  practically identical to the original nnU-Net (+0.07 on average, ahead on 3 of 5 folds), even closer than
  in the paper (+0.25). This is consistent with the paper's central claim that the Mamba-based model does not
  beat a well-configured CNN baseline. ResEnc M is ahead of all other models on every fold, but with five
  folds this is consistent, not statistically established.
- **SwinUNETR is clearly worse here** (−3.15 on average, lower on every fold) — a much larger gap than the
  paper's −0.25. The direction agrees with the paper (SwinUNETR below the CNNs), but the size does not, and
  this run uses a different SwinUNETR setup from the paper's own integration (see *Deviations*). I therefore
  report it as **not reproduced** and do not attribute the gap to the architecture itself. The largest loss is
  on the right ventricle (85.52 vs 90.12 for nnU-Net) and the myocardium (86.67 vs 89.96).

The hardest structures for all models are the myocardium (a thin ring) and the right ventricle (irregular
shape); the left-ventricle cavity is the easiest.

### Models as planned by nnU-Net (`3d_fullres`)

| Model | Network | Patch size | Batch | Target spacing (mm) | Encoder blocks per stage |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| nnU-Net (org.) | PlainConvUNet | 20 × 256 × 224 | 4 | 5 × 1.56 × 1.56 | 2, 2, 2, 2, 2, 2 |
| ResEnc M | ResidualEncoderUNet | 20 × 256 × 224 | 3 | 5 × 1.56 × 1.56 | 1, 3, 4, 6, 6, 6 |
| ResEnc L | ResidualEncoderUNet | 20 × 256 × 224 | 10 | 5 × 1.56 × 1.56 | 1, 3, 4, 6, 6, 6 |
| U-Mamba Bot | UMambaBot | 20 × 256 × 224 | 4 | 5 × 1.56 × 1.56 | nnU-Net default plan + Mamba block at the bottleneck |
| SwinUNETR | MONAI SwinUNETR | 32 × 256 × 224 (padded) | 3 | 5 × 1.56 × 1.56 | Swin Transformer encoder, feature size 48 |

The ResEnc L plan is identical to the one in the paper's Table 5 (patch 20 × 256 × 224, spacing
5 × 1.56 × 1.56, batch 10). U-Mamba's nnU-Net v2.1.1 produced the same default plan as nnU-Net v2.8.1
(patch, batch, spacing and number of stages all identical), so U-Mamba Bot and the original nnU-Net differ
only in the network.

## Repository layout

| Path | Step | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `scripts/download_acdc.py` | 1 | Resumable download of the ACDC ED/ES frames + labels from the official CREATIS Girder server (standard library only) |
| `scripts/step2_convert.sh` | 2 | Converts to nnU-Net format, writes the official 5-fold split, asserts no patient leakage, extracts the dataset fingerprint |
| `scripts/convert_acdc.py` | 2 | Runs nnU-Net's own `Dataset027_ACDC` converter, with a workaround for a v2.8.1 path bug (see *Deviations*) |
| `scripts/step3_plan_preprocess.sh` | 3 | Plans the three nnU-Net models and preprocesses `3d_fullres` once |
| `scripts/train_queue.sh` | 4 | Resumable training queue: one worker per GPU, 15 jobs (3 models × 5 folds), file locks, retry limit |
| `scripts/train_status.py` | 4 | Read-only status of all 15 jobs (epoch, s/epoch, ETA, best pseudo-Dice) |
| `scripts/evaluate_acdc.py` | 5 | Dice and NSD for the three nnU-Net models; writes `results/results_table.md` |
| `scripts/evaluate_all.py` | 5 | Dice and NSD for all five models in one table, plus the paired comparison; writes `results/results_table_all.md` |
| `scripts/make_figures.py` | 5 | The two figures in this README |
| `umamba/setup_umamba.sh` | U1 | Separate environment for U-Mamba (bundled nnU-Net 2.1.1), Mamba CUDA kernels, same split |
| `umamba/check_umamba.py` | U1 | GPU and installation checks (Mamba layer, SwinUNETR, paths, split) |
| `umamba/step2_umamba.sh` | U2 | Plans and preprocesses with nnU-Net 2.1.1 and compares the plan with the 2.8.1 runs |
| `umamba/smoke_umamba.py` | U2 | Memory and speed check of U-Mamba Bot / Enc and SwinUNETR before training |
| `umamba/train_queue_umamba.sh` | U3 | Resumable training queue for U-Mamba Bot / Enc and SwinUNETR |
| `umamba/train_status_umamba.py` | U3 | Read-only status of those jobs |
| `results/` | – | `results_table.md`, `results_table_all.md`, `results_summary*.json`, `figures/` |
| `env.sh.example` | – | Environment variables the nnU-Net scripts expect |

## Setup

Tested on Ubuntu 22.04, NVIDIA driver 535 (CUDA ≤ 12.2), so PyTorch is installed from the cu121 wheels.

```bash
conda create -n nnunet python=3.10 -y && conda activate nnunet
pip install torch --index-url https://download.pytorch.org/whl/cu121
pip install -r requirements.txt

mkdir -p ~/nnunet_repro && cp env.sh.example ~/nnunet_repro/env.sh   # then edit the paths
cp -r scripts ~/nnunet_repro/
source ~/nnunet_repro/env.sh
```

Environment the nnU-Net results were produced with: Python 3.10.20, PyTorch 2.5.1 (CUDA 12.1 build),
nnU-Net v2.8.1 (commit `468cf803df9b267150ae2b6c0c59b8ac84f16227`), 2× RTX 6000 Ada, driver 535.183.01,
32 CPU threads, 251 GB RAM.

U-Mamba and SwinUNETR ran in a separate environment cloned from it, with U-Mamba at commit `28459e3`
(bundled nnU-Net v2.1.1), `mamba_ssm` 2.2.4, `causal_conv1d` 1.5.0.post8, MONAI 1.3.0 and `transformers` 4.46.3.
`umamba/setup_umamba.sh` builds this environment.

## Data

ACDC is **not redistributed here**. It is available from the official challenge site
(<https://www.creatis.insa-lyon.fr/Challenge/acdc/>) under its own terms.

```bash
python ~/nnunet_repro/scripts/download_acdc.py --out ~/nnunet_repro/downloads/ACDC
```

The script skips the 4D cine volumes and the test set, which the paper's cross-validation does not use.

## Run

Each step is safe to re-run. Finished parts are skipped.

```bash
# nnU-Net (org.), ResEnc M, ResEnc L
bash ~/nnunet_repro/scripts/step2_convert.sh <ACDC root printed by the download script>
bash ~/nnunet_repro/scripts/step3_plan_preprocess.sh

# one worker per GPU, each inside its own tmux session so training survives a dropped VPN/SSH session
tmux new -s gpu0 'bash ~/nnunet_repro/scripts/train_queue.sh 0'
tmux new -s gpu1 'bash ~/nnunet_repro/scripts/train_queue.sh 1'
python ~/nnunet_repro/scripts/train_status.py           # check progress at any time

# U-Mamba Bot and SwinUNETR (separate environment, same raw data and split)
cp umamba/*.sh umamba/*.py ~/umamba_repro/
bash ~/umamba_repro/setup_umamba.sh
bash ~/umamba_repro/step2_umamba.sh
tmux new -s ub0 'bash ~/umamba_repro/train_queue_umamba.sh 0 bot swin'
tmux new -s ub1 'bash ~/umamba_repro/train_queue_umamba.sh 1 bot swin'
python ~/umamba_repro/train_status_umamba.py

# evaluation (nnU-Net environment) and figures
python ~/nnunet_repro/scripts/evaluate_all.py --out results     # works on finished folds only
python ~/nnunet_repro/scripts/make_figures.py --results results
```

Training resumes from `checkpoint_latest.pth` (`--c`), so a reboot or a lost connection costs at most
the epochs since the last checkpoint (saved every 50 epochs).

## Deviations from the paper

**nnU-Net models**

- **nnU-Net version.** I use v2.8.1. In that version, `nnunetv2.paths` returns path objects that
  `Dataset027_ACDC.make_out_dirs()` cannot handle (`AttributeError: '_EnvPath' object has no attribute
  'replace'`), so `convert_acdc.py` passes plain strings and then runs the converter's own logic unchanged.
  The split is the same official one.
- **Shared preprocessing.** The ResEnc presets reuse the default plans' `3d_fullres` preprocessed data
  (same target spacing and normalization), so preprocessing runs once.

**U-Mamba Bot and SwinUNETR**

- **Code and version.** Official U-Mamba repository (commit `28459e3`), which bundles nnU-Net v2.1.1. The
  paper does not state which implementation or version it used for these models.
- **Paths.** U-Mamba's `nnunetv2/paths.py` ignores the `nnUNet_raw` / `nnUNet_preprocessed` /
  `nnUNet_results` environment variables and uses a hard-coded folder; three lines were changed so it reads
  the environment variables. Nothing else in U-Mamba's code was changed.
- **Package versions.** `transformers` pinned to 4.46.3, because `mamba_ssm` 2.2.4 imports a name
  (`GreedySearchDecoderOnlyOutput`) that `transformers` 5.x removed.
- **Same data and split.** ACDC was preprocessed again with nnU-Net 2.1.1 from the same raw data; the
  official split file was copied over before training and checked to be byte-identical.
- **SwinUNETR setup (the likely source of its gap; none of these was tested separately):**
  - U-Mamba's SwinUNETR trainer is used as published: AdamW (lr 8e-4, weight decay 0.01), cosine schedule,
    no deep supervision, full precision (fp32), gradient clipping. The paper's authors integrated SwinUNETR
    into nnU-Net themselves; their exact recipe is not given, so it may differ.
  - The trainer pads the patch from 20 to 32 slices (SwinUNETR needs sizes divisible by 32).
  - **Batch size 3 instead of the planned 4**, because batch 4 in fp32 did not fit in 48 GB of GPU memory.
    This is done with a separate plans file (`nnUNetPlans_bs3`) that differs from the default plan only in the
    batch size.

**General**

- **Fewer models.** Five of the paper's configurations, chosen to fit the time budget.
- **Hardware and software.** RTX 6000 Ada instead of A100, and newer PyTorch / nnU-Net; training time and
  memory are therefore not directly comparable.

## Limitations

- One run per fold, as in the paper. Fold-to-fold SD is the only variance estimate; seed-to-seed
  variance was not measured, so the small U-Mamba Bot gap (−0.16 Dice) cannot be separated from run-to-run noise.
- The cause of the SwinUNETR gap was not isolated: training recipe, batch size and patch padding all differ
  from what the paper may have used, and testing them separately would need additional multi-day runs.
- Cross-validation on the training set only. Like the paper, there is no separate held-out test set.
- Five folds are too few to establish the ranking between the CNNs and U-Mamba statistically (see above).
- Results apply to ACDC and to these five configurations only.
